Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to SwiftCode QR Generator. Your privacy is highly important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information, data collection, and security rules while ensuring compliance with app store standards.

1. Data Collection and Usage

Our application is designed to function primarily as an offline utility. We respect your privacy and follow a strict data minimization policy:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, request, store, or share any personal identity information such as names, phone numbers, email addresses, or device locations.
  • Local Processing: All text, URLs, or inputs you enter to generate QR codes are processed entirely locally on your device. We do not have any remote server access to your data or generated QR codes.

2. Third-Party Services (Advertisements)

To keep this application free to use, we integrate third-party advertising services. Currently, we only use Google AdMob. These third-party networks may use device identifiers, cookies, or tracking technologies to serve relevant non-personalized or personalized advertisements based on your interests.

Google AdMob operates under its own separate privacy guidelines. You can read their framework here: Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Offline Capabilities and Security

Since the core engine of the app runs offline without transmitting data to external servers, your information is protected against unauthorized digital interceptions. The network permission required by the app is strictly used to display advertisements via Google AdMob when an active internet connection is available.

4. Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 3. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. If we discover that a child under 3 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our logs.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates made will be posted directly on this URL and will take effect immediately upon deployment.

6. Grievance Redressal & Contact Information

In accordance with the Information Technology Act and app store guidelines, if you have any questions, suggestions, or complaints regarding this Privacy Policy or app services, you may directly contact our designated Developer and Grievance Officer.
