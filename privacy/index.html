Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective: October 2026

Welcome to TypingTest (available at https://oxicpro.github.io/typing/). Your privacy is of the utmost importance to us. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect, how it is protected, how it is used, and your full rights over your data.

🔒 Strict Email Privacy Guarantee: Your email address (e.g. Gmail) is strictly confidential. It is never displayed publicly, never shown on the leaderboard, and never sold, rented, or shared with any third party. Only you have access to your personal login credentials.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide an accurate, high-performance typing test experience and track your progress:

  • Authentication & Profile Data: When you sign up or log in via Email or Google Sign-In, we securely store your User ID (UID), Email Address, and Chosen Username. Your username is the only identifier displayed publicly on the Top Typers leaderboard.
  • Typing Test Performance Metrics: We save your typing speed (Words Per Minute / WPM), accuracy percentage (%), test duration, and timestamps. This allows you to visualize your improvement on historical performance charts.
  • Community Blog Interactions: When you read or like blogs, we store anonymous or user-linked IDs to ensure each reader's view is counted only once and each person can cast only one heart like.
  • Local Device Preferences: User interface choices such as Dark Mode, Skip Error mode, Disable Backspace mode, and sound effects are saved locally in your browser's localStorage and are never transmitted to outside servers.

2. Email Confidentiality & Public Leaderboard Rules

We believe in total transparency and privacy-first design:

  • No Public Email Exposure: Other players and visitors on the website can never view your email address.
  • Display Name Only: On the public leaderboard ("Top Typers"), only your self-chosen Username and your typing score (WPM & Accuracy) are displayed.
  • Profile Name Updates: You have full control to change your public display name at any time from your settings panel.

3. Third-Party Advertisements & Google AdSense Policy

To keep this typing test 100% free for students and typists worldwide, our website may display third-party advertisements served by partners such as Google AdSense:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Personalized Advertising Opt-Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. Cookies & Web Technologies

We use cookies and browser storage technologies strictly for essential functionality:

  • Essential Cookies: Kept by Google Firebase Authentication to keep you securely signed in across browsing sessions.
  • Preferences Storage: Browser localStorage is used to remember your theme (Dark / Light) and typing options without sending data across the network.
  • Third-Party Advertising Cookies: Used by advertising partners as described in Section 3 above. You can disable cookies at any time via your browser settings.

5. Cloud Infrastructure & Security (Google Firebase)

All account data and typing scores are housed in secure, enterprise-grade cloud databases provided by Google Cloud Firestore. Data transmission is protected end-to-end using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS 256-bit encryption, preventing unauthorized interception, theft, or tampering.

6. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

Our typing test is an educational tool designed for general audiences, students, and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will delete the information promptly.

7. Your Data Rights & Deletion Requests

You retain complete ownership over your data:

  • You can inspect your saved test histories and graphs at any time in the 📈 History section.
  • You can update your username in real-time from the ⚙ Settings drawer.
  • If you wish to have your account, scores, or all associated records permanently erased from our Firebase database, simply contact us at our dedicated email below, and your request will be honored immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technological improvements, legal requirements, or new features. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
